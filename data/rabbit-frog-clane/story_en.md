## title

Water for the Serow

Water for the Serow

## description

In a dry summer, an old serow on top of a high cliff grew weak with thirst. This is the story of a rabbit, a frog, and a crane who work together to bring it water.

### speech

In a dry summer, an old serow on top of a high cliff grew weak with thirst. This is the story of a rabbit, a frog, and a crane who work together to bring it water.

## page_1

image: 1-first.png

It was a summer with no rain for many days, and even the little stream in the forest had dried up.
A small bird came and told them that an old serow, a wild mountain goat, lay weak with thirst on top of a high cliff.
The three of them decided to bring the serow some water.

### speech

It was a summer with no rain for many days, and even the little stream in the forest had dried up.

The three of them decided to bring the serow some water.

## page_2

image: 2-rabbit.png

The rabbit walked all through the forest with its ears and nose close to the ground.
At the foot of an old willow, it found a patch of soil that was cool and damp.

### speech

The rabbit walked all through the forest with its ears and nose close to the ground.
At the foot of an old willow, it found a patch of soil that was cool and damp.
When it dug with its front paws, clear water slowly began to seep up.

## page_3

image: 3-frog.png

The frog dived into the little pool and scooped out the mud and pebbles until the water ran clear.
Then, from the bottom, it brought up a large shell.

### speech

## page_4

image: 4-clane.png

It flew slowly toward the cliff, careful not to spill a drop.
On top of the cliff, the serow was lying in the shade of a rock.

### speech

## page_5

At the foot of the cliff, the rabbit and the frog looked up, relieved.
