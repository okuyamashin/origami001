# Image prompts: clane-rabbit-frog

各ページの画像は、`common` の style・characters・constraints と、そのページの scene・composition をつなげて1つのプロンプトにして使う。

## common

### style

Horizontal 3:2 illustration. A blend of an antique botanical plate and a natural-history animal illustration: precise, fine ink outlines, delicate cross-hatching, and meticulous colored-pencil shading. Leaf veins, ferns, reeds, bark, fur, and feathers are carefully rendered. A subtle texture of off-white paper. A palette of plant greens, ochre, grey-blue, and cream, with small, restrained accents of red and blue. Plants are part of a natural landscape in a story scene, not arranged like specimens. A quiet beauty that children can understand and adults can appreciate.

### characters

- The rabbit: a white rabbit with natural proportions, wearing a plain blue scarf.
- The crane: a red-crowned crane with natural proportions, carrying a small ochre shoulder bag on a strap.
- The frog: a small frog with natural proportions, olive-green skin with natural dark mottled markings, wearing a plain red scarf.
- The baby fireflies: small, segmented Genji firefly larvae, dark brown, each giving off a soft yellow-green glow from the tip of its body.
- All animals have natural anatomy, small eyes, closed mouths, and nearly neutral expressions.

### constraints

No watercolor bleeding or blur, no impasto, no glossy digital painting, no 3D rendering. Keep foreground animals and plants sharply drawn; mist, rain, or darkness only lowers contrast in the distance. No large eyes, no cartoon faces, no baby-like proportions, no exaggerated gestures. Include only the characters named in the scene. No bell. No text, labels, frames, watermarks, or signatures. No musical notes, sound lines, or other symbols.

## page_1

### scene

A spring night just after rain, at the edge of a forest. Wet leaves and young ferns glisten. Far below, deep in a dark valley, many tiny yellow-green lights twinkle. The crane, the rabbit, and the frog stand together on a mossy rise, looking down toward the lights. A thin crescent moon and a few stars.

### composition

A wide night landscape in deep blue-grey and green tones. The three companions are in the lower foreground, drawn clearly against the moonlit sky. The tiny lights are small points in the valley in the middle distance.

## page_2

### scene

The crane flies slowly in a circle high above a dark forest at night, its wings fully spread. Far below, at the bottom of a valley, a winding stream is marked by a line of tiny yellow-green lights.

### composition

Seen from slightly above and behind the crane. The crane's whole body with spread wings fills most of the width of the image. The valley and the line of lights are small, in the lower part of the image. Moonlight on the crane's white feathers. Only the crane appears among the three companions; the rabbit and the frog do not appear.

## page_3

### scene

A narrow, steep path going down into the valley at night, tangled with tree roots and ferns. The rabbit carefully steps down the path, ears raised, body low. Below, a stream glints faintly with soft yellow-green light coming from under the water.

### composition

A low, side-on close view. The rabbit's body takes up about two-thirds of the height of the image; its ears and careful posture stand out. The glowing stream is in the lower background. Only the rabbit appears among the three companions; the crane and the frog do not appear.

## page_4

### scene

Underwater in a clear, cold stream at night. The frog swims close to the bottom and looks under a flat stone. Several tiny firefly larvae cling to the underside of the stone, each glowing softly yellow-green. Pebbles, a few water plants, and small bubbles.

### composition

A close view at the level of the stream bed. The frog's whole body takes up about half the height of the image. The glowing larvae are clearly visible under the stone, small but distinct. Only the frog appears among the three companions; the rabbit and the crane do not appear.

## page_5

### scene

The mossy bank of the stream at night. A few firefly larvae crawl slowly up the bank, each glowing softly yellow-green, leaving the water. The rabbit, the crane, and the frog sit quietly side by side nearby, watching the little lights. Young ferns and spring flowers in the darkness.

### composition

A calm, wide view of the stream bank. The three companions are in the foreground, seen from the side. The glowing larvae are scattered on the bank in the middle ground. Gentle moonlight, deep blue-green night tones.
