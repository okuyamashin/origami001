## title

The Fawn in the Fog

### speech

The Fawn in the Fog

## description

On a foggy morning, a fawn got lost in the marsh. This is the story of a crane, a frog, and a rabbit who each do their part to bring the fawn back to its mother.

### speech

## page_1

image: 1-first.png

It was a morning when thick white fog covered the whole marsh.
At the edge of the marsh, a mother deer was calling again and again, looking for her fawn.
The crane, the frog, and the rabbit looked at one another and set off into the fog.

## page_2

image: 2-clane.png

The crane flew high, up above the fog.

Through a gap in the fog, it saw a small grassy island surrounded by water, and a fawn curled up on it.

## page_3

image: 3-frog.png

The frog swam across the marsh in the direction the crane had shown.
When it reached the grassy island, the fawn was trembling.
The frog felt around under the water, found a shallow path of stones, and led the fawn to the shore.

## page_4

Even on the shore, the fog was so thick that no one could tell which way to go.
The rabbit listened carefully with its long ears and picked out the mother deer's voice far away.
The rabbit walked ahead, and the fawn followed, one step at a time.
