# Image prompts: rabbit-frog-clane

各ページの画像は、`common` の style・characters・constraints と、そのページの scene・composition をつなげて1つのプロンプトにして使う。

## common

### style

Horizontal 3:2 illustration. A blend of an antique botanical plate and a natural-history animal illustration: precise, fine ink outlines, delicate cross-hatching, and meticulous colored-pencil shading. Leaf veins, ferns, reeds, bark, fur, and feathers are carefully rendered. A subtle texture of off-white paper. A palette of plant greens, ochre, grey-blue, and cream, with small, restrained accents of red and blue. Plants are part of a natural landscape in a story scene, not arranged like specimens. A quiet beauty that children can understand and adults can appreciate.

### characters

- The rabbit: a white rabbit with natural proportions, wearing a plain blue scarf.
- The crane: a red-crowned crane with natural proportions, carrying a small ochre shoulder bag on a strap.
- The frog: a small frog with natural proportions, olive-green skin with natural dark mottled markings, wearing a plain red scarf.
- The small bird: a Japanese white-eye with olive-green feathers and a white ring around the eye.
- The serow: an old Japanese serow, a wild goat-antelope with a shaggy dark grey-brown coat and short, backward-curving black horns.
- The shell: a large freshwater mussel shell, dark outside and pearly inside.
- All animals have natural anatomy, small eyes, closed mouths, and nearly neutral expressions.

### constraints

No watercolor bleeding or blur, no impasto, no glossy digital painting, no 3D rendering. Keep foreground animals and plants sharply drawn; mist, rain, or darkness only lowers contrast in the distance. No large eyes, no cartoon faces, no baby-like proportions, no exaggerated gestures. Include only the characters named in the scene. No bell. No text, labels, frames, watermarks, or signatures. No musical notes, sound lines, or other symbols.

## page_1

### scene

A hot, dry summer afternoon at the edge of a forest. A small stream bed has dried up, with cracked mud and bare pebbles. A small bird perches on a low branch. The rabbit, the frog, and the crane stand together beside the dry stream, looking up at the bird. Far in the background, a high, rocky cliff rises against a pale, hazy sky.

### composition

A wide landscape. The three companions and the bird are in the lower foreground. The high cliff is clearly visible in the distance. Bright, dry light; slightly faded summer greens.

## page_2

### scene

Inside the forest, at the foot of an old willow with exposed roots. The rabbit digs into dark, damp soil with its front paws. Clear water is beginning to seep up and gather in the small hollow it has dug.

### composition

A low, side-on close view. The rabbit's body takes up about two-thirds of the height of the image; its ears, head, and digging front paws stand out clearly. The willow roots and the small hollow of water are in the foreground. Only the rabbit appears among the three companions; the crane and the frog do not appear.

## page_3

### scene

A small, muddy pool under the willow roots. The frog is half in the water, pushing mud and pebbles out of the pool so the water turns clear. Beside the frog, a large freshwater mussel shell, pearly inside, lies at the bottom of the clear water.

### composition

A low viewpoint close to the water surface. The frog's whole body takes up about half the height of the image. The contrast between swirling mud and clear water is visible. The shell is clearly seen in the water. Only the frog appears among the three companions; the rabbit and the crane do not appear.

## page_4

### scene

The crane flies toward a high, rocky cliff, holding the shell full of water gently in its beak. On top of the cliff, small in the distance, an old serow lies in the shade of a rock. Dry grasses and a few pine trees cling to the cliff.

### composition

The crane's whole body with spread wings fills most of the width of the image, seen from the side. The cliff and the serow are small, in the upper right. Clear summer afternoon light. Only the crane appears among the three companions; the rabbit and the frog do not appear.

## page_5

### scene

Evening on top of the cliff. The serow stands up beside the shell of water, having drunk from it. The crane stands quietly beside it. Far below, at the foot of the cliff, the rabbit and the frog look up. The sky glows with the colors of sunset.

### composition

A tall, wide view showing both the cliff top and its foot. The serow and the crane are in the upper part, the rabbit and the frog small in the lower part. Warm, soft evening light.
