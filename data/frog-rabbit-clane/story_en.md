## title

To See the Sea

### speech

To See the Sea

## description

The three animals of the forest had never seen the sea. This is the story of a frog, a rabbit, and a crane who follow a river on a journey to see it.

### speech

The three animals of the forest had never seen the sea. This is the story of a frog, a rabbit, and a crane who follow a river on a journey to see it.

## page_1

image: 1-first.png

The three animals of the forest had never once seen the sea.
They had heard that if a river keeps flowing, its water reaches the sea at last.
On a morning at the end of summer, the three of them set off along the river.

## page_2

image: 2-frog.png

The river split into many branches, and no one could tell which one led to the sea.
The frog dived into the water and felt with its whole body which current was the strongest.
Following that current, the three of them traveled on for many days.

## page_4

image: 4-clane.png

Beyond the field, high sand dunes rose one after another.
The crane beat its great wings and rose above the dunes.
On the other side, it saw water as wide as could be, the same color as the sky.
