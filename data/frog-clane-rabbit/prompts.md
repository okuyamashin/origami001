# Image prompts: frog-clane-rabbit

各ページの画像は、`common` の style・characters・constraints と、そのページの scene・composition をつなげて1つのプロンプトにして使う。

## common

### style

Horizontal 3:2 illustration. A blend of an antique botanical plate and a natural-history animal illustration: precise, fine ink outlines, delicate cross-hatching, and meticulous colored-pencil shading. Leaf veins, ferns, reeds, bark, fur, and feathers are carefully rendered. A subtle texture of off-white paper. A palette of plant greens, ochre, grey-blue, and cream, with small, restrained accents of red and blue. Plants are part of a natural landscape in a story scene, not arranged like specimens. A quiet beauty that children can understand and adults can appreciate.

### characters

- The rabbit: a white rabbit with natural proportions, wearing a plain blue scarf.
- The crane: a red-crowned crane with natural proportions, carrying a small ochre shoulder bag on a strap.
- The frog: a small frog with natural proportions, olive-green skin with natural dark mottled markings, wearing a plain red scarf.
- The skylarks: Eurasian skylarks, small brown streaked birds with a short crest; two adults and three fluffy chicks that cannot yet fly far.
- The field mice: a family of small brown field mice, two adults and a few young.
- All animals have natural anatomy, small eyes, closed mouths, and nearly neutral expressions.

### constraints

No watercolor bleeding or blur, no impasto, no glossy digital painting, no 3D rendering. Keep foreground animals and plants sharply drawn; mist, rain, or darkness only lowers contrast in the distance. No large eyes, no cartoon faces, no baby-like proportions, no exaggerated gestures. Include only the characters named in the scene. No bell. No text, labels, frames, watermarks, or signatures. No musical notes, sound lines, or other symbols.

## page_1

### scene

A hot, humid early-summer afternoon in a meadow beside a river. Tall grasses, clover, and wildflowers. A skylark family sits in the grass near their ground nest, and a family of field mice peeks out from among the stems. The frog, the crane, and the rabbit rest along a narrow path in the meadow. A hazy sky; a low hill rises at the edge of the meadow.

### composition

A wide landscape. The three companions are in the lower foreground. The skylarks and field mice are small but clearly visible in the middle ground. The hill is visible in the background. Heavy, humid light.

## page_2

### scene

At the edge of the river in the meadow, the frog sits on a wet stone at the water's edge. Its throat is puffed out as it calls. The grasses around it bend in a sudden, cool, damp wind, and the sky above is beginning to darken.

### composition

A low viewpoint close to the water surface. The frog's whole body takes up about half the height of the image, with its puffed throat clearly visible. Bending grasses frame the frog. Only the frog appears among the three companions; the rabbit and the crane do not appear.

## page_3

### scene

High above the river valley, the crane flies upstream with its wings fully spread. Ahead, beyond the mountains, dark storm clouds are rolling in. Below, the river is swollen and brown.

### composition

The crane's whole body with spread wings fills most of the width of the image, seen from the side. The dark clouds and the mountains are in the background, the swollen river small below. Dramatic but restrained light before the storm. Only the crane appears among the three companions; the rabbit and the frog do not appear.

## page_4

### scene

On a grassy hill under a darkening sky, the rabbit leads the way toward the entrance of an old burrow among the roots of a hawthorn bush. Behind the rabbit, the skylark family hops along and the field mice follow in a line.

### composition

A low, side-on close view. The rabbit's body takes up about two-thirds of the height of the image, at the front of the line. The skylarks and field mice are smaller behind it. The burrow entrance is visible ahead. Only the rabbit appears among the three companions; the crane and the frog do not appear.

## page_5

### scene

Morning after the storm. The meadow below the hill is partly flooded, with water slowly going down and grass glistening. A large rainbow arches across the clearing sky. On the hilltop by the burrow, the skylarks and the field mice have come out, and the frog, the crane, and the rabbit stand together with them, quietly looking out over the meadow.

### composition

A wide, calm view from the hilltop. The companions and the small animals are in the foreground, seen from behind or from the side. The meadow and the rainbow fill the background. Fresh, clear morning light.
