# Image prompts: frog-rabbit-clane

各ページの画像は、`common` の style・characters・constraints と、そのページの scene・composition をつなげて1つのプロンプトにして使う。

## common

### style

Horizontal 3:2 illustration. A blend of an antique botanical plate and a natural-history animal illustration: precise, fine ink outlines, delicate cross-hatching, and meticulous colored-pencil shading. Leaf veins, ferns, reeds, bark, fur, and feathers are carefully rendered. A subtle texture of off-white paper. A palette of plant greens, ochre, grey-blue, and cream, with small, restrained accents of red and blue. Plants are part of a natural landscape in a story scene, not arranged like specimens. A quiet beauty that children can understand and adults can appreciate.

### characters

- The rabbit: a white rabbit with natural proportions, wearing a plain blue scarf.
- The crane: a red-crowned crane with natural proportions, carrying a small ochre shoulder bag on a strap.
- The frog: a small frog with natural proportions, olive-green skin with natural dark mottled markings, wearing a plain red scarf.
- All animals have natural anatomy, small eyes, closed mouths, and nearly neutral expressions.

### constraints

No watercolor bleeding or blur, no impasto, no glossy digital painting, no 3D rendering. Keep foreground animals and plants sharply drawn; mist, rain, or darkness only lowers contrast in the distance. No large eyes, no cartoon faces, no baby-like proportions, no exaggerated gestures. Include only the characters named in the scene. No bell. No text, labels, frames, watermarks, or signatures. No musical notes, sound lines, or other symbols.

## page_1

### scene

An early morning at the end of summer, on the bank of a forest river. Late-summer grasses, a few early yellow leaves. The frog, the rabbit, and the crane stand together on the riverbank, ready to set off downstream. The river flows away into the distance.

### composition

A wide landscape. The three companions are in the lower foreground, facing downstream. The river leads the eye into the distance. Clear, fresh morning light.

## page_2

### scene

A place where the river splits into many shallow branches among gravel bars and reeds. The frog is in the water of one branch, its body stretched against the current, feeling which way the water flows strongest. Ripples curl around the frog.

### composition

A low viewpoint close to the water surface. The frog's whole body takes up about half the height of the image. The branching channels spread out behind the frog. Only the frog appears among the three companions; the rabbit and the crane do not appear.

## page_3

### scene

A wide field of tall grasses and wildflowers where the river fades away. The rabbit stands up among the grass with its ears raised, turned toward a sound far away. Grass seeds and stems are drawn in fine detail.

### composition

A low, side-on close view. The rabbit's body takes up about two-thirds of the height of the image; its raised ears stand out against the sky. The tall grass surrounds it. Only the rabbit appears among the three companions; the crane and the frog do not appear.

## page_4

### scene

High sand dunes covered here and there with beach grass. The crane rises above the top of a dune with its wings fully spread. Beyond the dunes, for the first time, the wide sea is visible, the same pale blue as the sky.

### composition

The crane's whole body with spread wings fills most of the width of the image, seen from behind and slightly to the side, so the sea beyond is visible. The sea stretches across the horizon. Only the crane appears among the three companions; the rabbit and the frog do not appear.

## page_5

### scene

Evening on a quiet beach. Gentle waves come in and go out over the sand. The frog, the rabbit, and the crane stand side by side near the edge of the water, looking out at the sea as the sun sets. Footprints and small shells on the sand.

### composition

A wide, calm view. The three companions are in the foreground, seen from behind. The sea and the setting sun fill the background. Warm, golden evening light.
