# Image prompts: clane-frog-rabbit

各ページの画像は、`common` の style・characters・constraints と、そのページの scene・composition をつなげて1つのプロンプトにして使う。

## common

### style

Horizontal 3:2 illustration. A blend of an antique botanical plate and a natural-history animal illustration: precise, fine ink outlines, delicate cross-hatching, and meticulous colored-pencil shading. Leaf veins, ferns, reeds, bark, fur, and feathers are carefully rendered. A subtle texture of off-white paper. A palette of plant greens, ochre, grey-blue, and cream, with small, restrained accents of red and blue. Plants are part of a natural landscape in a story scene, not arranged like specimens. A quiet beauty that children can understand and adults can appreciate.

### characters

- The rabbit: a white rabbit with natural proportions, wearing a plain blue scarf.
- The crane: a red-crowned crane with natural proportions, carrying a small ochre shoulder bag on a strap.
- The frog: a small green frog with natural proportions, wearing a plain red scarf.
- The fawn: a young sika deer fawn with a reddish-brown coat and white spots.
- The mother deer: an adult female sika deer without antlers.
- All animals have natural anatomy, small eyes, closed mouths, and nearly neutral expressions.

### constraints

No watercolor bleeding or blur, no impasto, no glossy digital painting, no 3D rendering. No large eyes, no cartoon faces, no baby-like proportions, no exaggerated gestures. No text, labels, frames, watermarks, or signatures. No musical notes, sound lines, or other symbols.

## page_1

### scene

Early morning at the edge of a wide marsh covered in thick white fog. Reeds and sedges line the shore, and an old willow is faintly visible in the fog. In the middle distance, a mother deer stands half-hidden in the fog with her head raised, calling. The crane, the frog, and the rabbit stand together on a mossy bank, looking toward the fog, about to set off.

### composition

A wide landscape. The three companions are in the lower foreground, small relative to the scene. The mother deer is in the middle distance, softened by the fog. Soft, diffused morning light.

## page_2

### scene

The crane flies high above a sea of white fog with its wings fully spread. Treetops poke out of the fog here and there, and the early sun is low on the horizon. Below, through a gap in the fog, a small grassy island surrounded by water can be seen, with a tiny fawn curled up on it.

### composition

The crane's whole body with spread wings fills most of the width of the image. The island and the fawn are small, in the lower part of the image, seen through the gap in the fog. Only the crane appears among the three companions; the rabbit and the frog do not appear.

## page_3

### scene

The frog is half in the shallow water of the foggy marsh, beside a line of flat stones just below the surface, leading from a small grassy island toward the shore. Behind the frog, the fawn carefully steps onto the first stone, its legs trembling. Drops of water cling to the frog's skin.

### composition

A low viewpoint close to the water surface. The frog's whole body is in the foreground and takes up about half the height of the image. The fawn is smaller, in the middle ground. The line of stones leads the eye from the island toward the shore. Only the frog appears among the three companions; the rabbit and the crane do not appear.

## page_4

### scene

A misty path on the shore among ferns and grasses. The rabbit sits up alert with its long ears raised, turned toward a faint sound far away in the fog. The fawn waits just behind the rabbit, ready to follow.

### composition

A low, side-on close view. The rabbit's body takes up about two-thirds of the height of the image, and its ears and head stand out clearly. The fawn is partly visible behind the rabbit, drawn more lightly. The mother deer does not appear. Only the rabbit appears among the three companions; the crane and the frog do not appear.

## page_5

### scene

The morning sun has risen, and the fog is clearing into thin wisps. The meadow at the edge of the marsh glistens with dew. The fawn presses close to its mother. The rabbit, the crane, and the frog stand side by side at a little distance, quietly watching them.

### composition

A wide, calm landscape. The mother deer and the fawn are in the middle ground. The three companions are in the foreground, seen from the side or from behind. Warm, clear morning light.
